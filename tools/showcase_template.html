Animescene Fight Panels

Every panel below was staged in 3D first: bodies posed with exact inverse kinematics, blows placed on the body part they hit, reactions moved to where the blow landed. Only then did an anime SDXL model paint it, guided by pose, depth and per-character regions. Each character's identity comes from its own generated turnaround sheet, and wounds are drawn by code at a fixed spot on the body, so they come back identical in every later panel.

Cast memory

Identity sheets and recorded wounds

Generated once per character. Every panel attends to the sheet inside that character's own 3D region only. Wounds are stored as body-surface coordinates (bone, position along it, angle around it).

How it works

What the code guarantees, and what the model paints

Contacts are geometry

A palm strike places the heel of the hand on the stomach surface and dents it in the depth map. A back-of-knee kick mounts the attacker behind the victim at the exact leg reach. Headlocks put the forearm under the throat.

Reactions follow the blow

Doubling over, stumbling, knee buckling and being knocked flying are solved as the victim's pose and then moved so the struck part sits where the blow landed, never pulled toward the attacker.

Identity per region

Each pixel can attend only to its own character's turnaround sheet. Four fighters keep four faces, outfits and palettes without bleeding into each other.

Wounds are persistent decals

Seeded patterns re-projected through the posed body, hidden when the spot faces away or is covered. The image model is never asked to invent injuries.

Rendered on a 4-core CPU (bf16) with WAI-Illustrious-SDXL, ControlNet-Union ProMax and sdxl-vae-fp16-fix. Source code on GitHub
